# Demand from ESL Tutors and Adult Learners for Job-Interview English Lessons, and Which Job-Specific Topics Are Requested Most

Method note: research was done on 2026-11-14 through web search. Fetching full pages failed for preply.com, en.islcollective.com and eslbrains.com because those hosts are blocked by this session's network settings, so the evidence for those sites comes from search snippets only. No Reddit threads appeared in any search, including `site:reddit.com` queries. As a result, **direct Reddit evidence is missing** (see Gaps). View, download and rating counts from ISL Collective, BusyTeacher and similar sites could not be retrieved.

## 1. Teaching-material sites: what job-interview lessons exist, which roles they cover, and how popular they are

### Takeaway
The free and freemium sites carry many **generic** job-interview lessons, at levels from A2 to C2. They cover common questions, phrases, body language, online interviews, the STAR method and "what not to do". **Role-specific** interview lessons are rare. The few that exist are a nurse example inside a general Onestopenglish lesson, a technical software-engineering listening activity on Twee, and an "Interview at a Hotel" listening task in a Canadian LINC workbook. No popularity metrics could be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- ESL Brains has a B2 lesson, "How to do an online job interview right". In it, students discuss the pros and cons of online interviews and match half-phrases about interviewing online. — [ESL Brains](https://eslbrains.com/?p=94360) (summary from a search snippet)
- Linguahouse has several generic job-interview lessons. One is A2–B1 (applying for a job, with the modal "should"), one is B1–B2 (interview behaviour and what not to do) and one is B2–C1 (the job interview, ending in a role play). Search found no nurse or hotel versions. — [Linguahouse lesson PDF hosted on Lyon 1 Moodle](https://moodle.univ-lyon1.fr/mod/resource/view.php?id=324921); [TUL e-learning copy](https://elearning.tul.cz/mod/resource/view.php?id=615271)
- Onestopenglish has a lesson called "Language for job interviews" with a worksheet, teacher's notes and video, tagged B1–C1. Its example profile is a **nurse** ("sociable, sympathetic; ability to work under pressure, good time management"). — [Onestopenglish](https://onestopenglish.com/language-for-/language-for-job-interviews/558165.article)
- Twinkl has an "ESL Useful Interview Phrases Activity Sheet [Adults, B1-B2]", which is generic. — [Twinkl](https://www.twinkl.com/resource/esl-useful-interview-phrases-worksheet-adults-b1-b2-t-e-1678090982)
- TES has a pack of 11 interviewing worksheets with answers, covering the STAR technique and the skills behind each question. It also lists a "Business English Interviews Level 3" lesson (B1+, 60–90 minutes, PDF and Google Slides). — [TES worksheets](https://www.tes.com/teaching-resource/interviewing-worksheets-with-answers-12037618); [TES Business English interviews](https://www.tes.com/en-us/teaching-resource/interviews-business-english-level-3-12114448)
- BusyTeacher has "ESL Job Interview: What To Be Prepared For", which is generic. — [BusyTeacher](https://busyteacher.org/4822-esl-job-interview-be-prepared.html)
- British Council LearnEnglish has a listening lesson, "A job interview", with a preparation task, audio, exercises and a transcript. Its comment section is very large, with dozens of indexed comment permalinks. This is a weak sign of high learner engagement. — [LearnEnglish "A job interview"](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/comment/138185)
- ESL Pals has generic interview lessons at B1, B2 and C1–C2. They include video discussion, interview practice questions, a "what not to say" warm-up and interview phrasal verbs. — [ESL Pals B1](https://eslpals.com/business-english/B1/esl-business-lesson-job-interview); [B2](https://eslpals.com/business-english/B2/esl-lesson-interview-business-b2); [C1–C2](https://eslpals.com/business-english/C1-C2/esl-lesson-interview-process)
- Twee lists several interview lessons, including "Job Interview: Perfect Fit" and "Job Interview: Self-Presentation Skills" (B1–B2). It also has a listening activity based on a **technical interview for a software engineering position**, with vocabulary such as "debug". — [Twee jobs lessons](https://twee.com/esl-lesson-plans/job); [Twee p.2](https://twee.com/esl-lesson-plans/job?page=2); [Self-Presentation](https://twee.com/esl-lesson-plans/job-interview-self-presentation-skills)
- EnglishPost.org has a "tips for a job interview" lesson whose sample answers are written for a **software developer** (development courses, becoming a tech lead). — [EnglishPost](https://englishpost.org/lesson-plans/lesson-tips-for-a-job-interview)
- ESL Printables has several teacher-made interview worksheets. These include vocabulary and tips gap-fills, listening on asking and answering interview questions, and an elementary role-play. — [ESL Printables Job interview category](https://www.eslprintables.com/Speaking_worksheets/Job_interview); [example](https://www.eslprintables.com/printable.asp?id=253809)
- Other generic interview lessons: ESLFlow (an interviewer and applicant role-play), Fluentize ("Job interview AI", B1, behind a paywall), Amerilingua ("Job Interview Fails") and a Devil Wears Prada scene lesson at A2. — [ESLFlow](https://www.eslflow.com/Job-interview-lesson.html); [Fluentize](https://app.fluentize.com/lesson/job-interview-ai); [Amerilingua](https://www.amerilingua.com/ja/esl-lesson-plans/job-interview-fails); [FluentU roundup](https://fluentu.com/blog/educator-english/esl-lesson-plans-for-adults)
- A tourism-sector interview unit exists in a commercial textbook: "Unit 3 Navigating the Job Interview Process in the Tourism Sector". — [izzi.digital](https://bg.izzi.digital/DOS/418317/744021.html)

### Inferences
- Coverage is concentrated on **generic interview language**: "tell me about yourself", strengths and weaknesses, polite phrases, body language, online and video interviews, and STAR. The market for generic material looks saturated.
- Where a job role does appear, it is usually an example inside a generic lesson, such as the Onestopenglish nurse or the EnglishPost developer. It is rarely the organising theme of a full dialogue lesson.
- The roles that most often have *some* coverage are nursing and healthcare, software and IT, and hotel or tourism. Retail, care work, warehouse and logistics, trades and engineering, finance and teaching barely appear as dedicated ESL interview lessons in the results.

### Gaps
- No view, download or rating counts could be retrieved for ISL Collective, BusyTeacher, ESL Library, Linguahouse or ESL Brains, because direct fetches were blocked and snippets did not include them. Popularity ranking by site metrics therefore remains unverified.
- No job-interview lessons from Breaking News English or ESL Library surfaced in the searches.
- ISL Collective's catalogue could not be inspected, since both search and fetch failed.

## 2. Tutor marketplaces (Preply, iTalki, Cambly, AmazingTalker): how prominent interview preparation is, and which professions appear

### Takeaway
"Job interview prep" is an established filter category or landing page on Preply and AmazingTalker. Preply also sells a dedicated "English for Interviews" course. Preply's interview-prep tutor listing runs past page 1,300. Tutors usually combine interview prep with Business English and IELTS or TOEFL preparation. On iTalki, some teachers brand themselves as interview specialists. No Cambly data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Preply has a dedicated listing, "tutors-english-job-interview-prep", and indexed pages go beyond page 1,300 (for example ?page=1308). A snippet reported "12,586 English teachers available". Prices shown range from about $3 to $40+ per lesson. — [Preply interview prep tutors](https://preply.com/en/online/tutors-english-job-interview-prep?page=2); [page 1308](https://preply.com/en/online/tutors-english-job-interview-prep?page=1308)
- Preply also has an "interview-preparation" classes listing that runs past page 240. — [Preply interview-preparation classes](https://preply.com/en/classes/english/interview-preparation); [page 246](https://preply.com/en/classes/english/interview-preparation?page=246)
- Preply sells an "English for Interviews" course covering interview vocabulary, answering techniques and understanding job descriptions in English. — [Preply course](https://preply.com/en/courses/english/english-for-interviews-course?page=7)
- Preply tutor self-descriptions combine interview prep with business and finance and IELTS or TOEFL. One example reads "Certified English teacher, specialize in business: finance/econ, Interview Prep and TOEFL/IELTS Prep". One tutor describes mock interviews for **education, community and social services** roles. — [Preply listing snippet](https://preply.com/en/online/tutors-english-job-interview-prep?page=18); [Preply tutor profile](https://preply.com/en/tutor/3316780)

### Inferences
- The size of Preply's interview-prep listing points to strong supply. It also suggests that "job interview" is one of the platform's standard learner goals. However, the listing probably includes many tutors who simply ticked the box, so its size is a weak demand proxy.
- In tutor profiles found via search, interview prep is mostly framed through **business, finance and corporate** roles, and sometimes through education and social services. Snippets did not show tutors pitching nursing, hospitality or trades interview prep.

### Gaps
- Exact counts of tutors per specialty, and any learner-goal statistics, could not be verified because preply.com was blocked from fetching.
- No Cambly information on interview specialties was found.
- No marketplace data broken down by profession (nursing, IT, hospitality and so on) was found.

## 3. What learners and teachers ask for on Reddit and forums, and which job-specific interview lessons are hard to find

### Takeaway
Reddit content could not be retrieved, because search returned no Reddit pages. Forum evidence from nursing communities shows **anxiety among internationally educated nurses** about English-language interviews. Specialist sites also show demand for caregiver and care-worker interview practice. This is indirect evidence that healthcare and care roles are the strongest role-specific need.

### Cited Findings
- On Incredible Health, an international new-grad nurse who speaks English as a second language says they are "really nervous" about a job interview. — [Incredible Health nurse Q&A](https://www.incrediblehealth.com/nurse-advice/questions/ba5f77c0/hi-i-am-a-new-grad-an-international-nurse-and)
- An allnurses thread, "First RN Job and Anxious About Speaking English", shows a nurse worried about communication mistakes, and others reply that they share the fear. — [allnurses](https://allnurses.com/first-rn-job-anxious-about-t706190)
- OET (the Occupational English Test) publishes nursing interview tips and career-interview preparation posts aimed at international healthcare professionals. — [OET nursing interview tips](https://oet.com/post/nursing-interview-tips); [OET career interview preparation](https://oet.com/post/oet-career-interview-preparation); [OET NHS recruitment](https://oet.com/post/nhs-recruitment-nurses-interview)
- NHS international recruitment uses **competency-based questions** and questions about **NHS Values**. Candidates are told to practise answers in a STAR(R) structure, and some recruiters run interviews over Skype or video. — [NHS Professionals application pack](https://www.nhsprofessionals.nhs.uk/-/media/corporate/nhsp-international/nursing-and-midwifery-application-support-pack.pdf); [ID Medical international nurse guide](https://www.id-medical.com/international-nurses-guide-to-preparing-for-your-nhs-nursing-interview-5-questions-with-example-answers/); [Sanctuary Personnel](https://www.sanctuarypersonnel.com/blog/2020/04/how-do-international-candidates-prepare-for-a-nursing-interview)
- SpeakShark has a niche guide, "caregiver job interview English practice (UK)". It covers why you want to work in care, dignity and person-centred care, and STAR answers. — [SpeakShark](https://speakshark.com/blog/caregiver-job-interview-english-practice-uk)
- Care-worker interview guides from employers and recruiters focus on judgement, communication, person-centred care, handling emergencies and "what does a care worker do". These guides are not written for ESL learners. — [Randstad UK](https://www.randstad.co.uk/career-advice/career-guidance/top-care-worker-interview-questions-answers-how-to-prepare-for-a-job/); [Home Instead](https://homeinstead.co.uk/recruitment/care-interview-questions); [High Speed Training](https://highspeedtraining.co.uk/hub/?p=61580)
- Mainstream ESL brands such as the British Council (IELTS blog) and Lingoda publish "job interviews in English as a non-native speaker" content. This suggests a general learner need. — [British Council TakeIELTS](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/blog/job-interviews-in-english-success-as-a-non-native-speaker); [Lingoda](https://blog.lingoda.com/en/4-steps-to-a-confident-job-interview-in-english/)
- Canadian settlement content addresses nervousness about English in interviews. — [LiveLearn.ca](https://livelearn.ca/article/employment/nervous-about-your-english-tips-to-help-you-express-yourself-well-in-an-interview)
- Shadowing-practice sites build lessons from nursing interview videos, for example "nursing interview questions hiring managers actually ask". — [ShadowingEnglish](https://shadowingenglish.com/practice/nursing-interview-questions-hiring-managers-actually-ask-indeed-GDBH3-JAFk8)

### Inferences
- Healthcare is the role-specific segment where learner anxiety, a high-stakes recruitment pipeline (NHS, OET and NMC) and commercial interest all meet. Nursing and care work are the clearest roles to prioritise.
- Interview prep for care workers and caregivers exists mainly as employer content in native-speaker English, plus a few niche apps. Free ESL-graded dialogue lessons for these roles appear thin.

### Gaps
- **No Reddit threads** from r/EnglishLearning, r/TEFL, r/ESL_Teachers or r/ESL could be found. The search index did not return Reddit, and fetching was not possible. The questions "what do teachers ask for on Reddit" and "which specific lessons are hard to find" remain unverified from community sources.
- No forum evidence for hospitality, IT or trades interview anxiety among ESL speakers was found in this pass.

## 4. ESOL employability curricula (UK, US IET, Canada LINC/CLB, Australia AMEP): which sectors and interview skills they cover

### Takeaway
Government-funded adult ESOL employability provision is concentrated in **healthcare (especially nurse aide, CNA and care homes), hospitality and catering, retail and customer service, and manufacturing**. Interview skills taught include predicting employer questions, describing experience and personality, references, tone and body language, telephone and tiered interviews, and mock interviews with feedback.

### Cited Findings
- **US IET:** A CLASP national survey found that healthcare occupations dominate IET programmes, and that over half of IET programmes prepare learners for the **Certified Nursing Assistant** credential. Note that the survey is from about 2017. — [CLASP IET brief](https://www.clasp.org/publications/report/brief/integrated-education-and-training-career-pathways-policy-practice/)
- US IET examples: Hawkeye College runs I-BEST programmes for English language learners in **CNA, CNC machining and Hospitality**. Wake Tech offers **Nurse Aide, EKG Technician and Central Sterile Processing** IETs. Virginia's VALRC has curricula for **EMT, Foundational Skills in Healthcare, Manufacturing and Mechanical Maintenance**. A COABE session names **healthcare, manufacturing, construction, hospitality and IT**. — [Hawkeye](https://www.hawkeyecollege.edu/community/adult-education/integrated-education-and-training); [Wake Tech IETs](https://waketech.edu/sites/default/files/program-page-files/IETs%20PY%202022-23.pdf); [VALRC IET Blueprint](https://valrc.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/IETBlueprint_a.pdf); [COABE resource](https://adultedresource.coabe.org/subject-area/integrated-education-and-training/page/7)
- Oregon published an "IETs in Oregon 2026" document, which could provide a current sector mix, but its content was not retrieved. — [Oregon HECC](https://www.oregon.gov/highered/about/community-colleges-workforce-development/Documents/abs-resources/IETs%20in%20Oregon%202026.pdf)
- **UK:** WEA runs an "ESOL Employability – Care Homes" course covering medication, personal care, recording patient information and care-setting vocabulary. — [WEA](https://wea.org.uk/courses/esol/8-october-esol-employability-care-homes)
- City Lit runs an Entry 3/Level 1 course in which learners predict employer questions, practise answers, work on stress and pronunciation, and role-play interview situations. It also runs a Level 1–2 course on customer care and **front-of-house hospitality**. — [City Lit workplace communication](https://www.citylit.ac.uk/courses/workplace-communication-skills-how-to-get-a-better-job-esol-entry-3-level-1)
- Halesowen's ESOL Skills for Employment (Entry 3) pairs ESOL with a vocational subject: **Health & Social Care, Business & Retail, or Hospitality & Catering**. Haringey's course lets learners specialise in **retail, care** or another vocational area. — [Halesowen](https://www.halesowen.ac.uk/study/course/esol-skills-for-employment-entry-level-3); [Haringey](https://new.haringey.gov.uk/jobs-training/haringey-learns/courses/search/esol-skills-life-work-entry-3-employability-skills)
- London's ESOL Plus Employment Hub adds customer service and hospitality content to traditional ESOL. — [London.gov.uk ESOL Plus](https://www.london.gov.uk/programmes-strategies/communities-and-social-justice/migrants-and-refugees/english-esol-classes-london/esol-plus-employment-hub)
- Enable's employability workshop covers references, talking about relevant experience, describing personality, and interview vocabulary. UCG's English for Work runs mock interviews and employer engagement. — [Enable](https://www.enable.uk.net/employability); [UCG](https://ucg.ac.uk/course/english-work-esol)
- **Canada LINC/CLB:** NorQuest's LINC Works "Interviews" modules (CLB 3 and CLB 5) are open interactive Pressbooks with H5P activities. CLB 3 covers talking about skills and experience and preparing for an interview in Canada. CLB 5 adds appropriate tone, language and body language. The CLB 3 module includes a listening task, "**Interview at a Hotel**". — [CLB 3 workbook](https://pressbooks.openeducationalberta.ca/norquest-lincworks-interviews-clb3/chapter/chapter-1/); [Interview at a Hotel](https://openeducationalberta.ca/norquest-lincworks-interviews-clb3/?p=64); [CLB 5 workbook](https://pressbooks.openeducationalberta.ca/norquest-lincworks-interviews-clb5/chapter/pre-lesson-reflection/); [NorQuest LINC Works](https://www.norquest.ca/about-us/research-and-innovation/research-and-innovation-projects/linc-works/curriculum-materials)
- The LINC 4/5 curriculum guidelines suggest **telephone and tiered interviews**, role-playing a request for an interview in person or by phone, and videotaping practice interviews. — [Settlement.org LINC 4 Job Interviews](https://settlement.org/downloads/linc/LCG1to5/Tm9LINC4.pdf)
- **Australia AMEP:** The Pathways to Employment Program (PEP) is a 200-hour add-on. At TAFE NSW it comprises 160 hours of English for workplace communication and job seeking (résumés and interview skills) plus 40 hours of work experience. The AMEP Work Experience Program (AWEP) offers 200 extra hours, including 80 hours of work experience. AMES Australia's "3 Steps to Employment" has an Interview module with mock interviews and immediate feedback. — [TAFE NSW PEP](https://tafensw.edu.au/student-services/adult-migrant-english-program-amep/pep); [AWEP](https://production.nmt.doghouse.cloud/amep-work-experience-program-awep); [AMES 3 Steps](https://ames.net.au/courses/3-steps-to-employment); [Home Affairs PEP employer factsheet](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/amep-subsite/Files/pathway-to-employment-info-for-employers-factsheet.pdf)
- New Zealand's Victoria University "Language in the Workplace" eldercare units give care-setting dialogues, for example a worker telling a resident what she is about to do. This is useful vocabulary, though the units are not interview-focused. — [VUW eldercare unit 1](https://wgtn.ac.nz/lals/centres-and-institutes/language-in-the-workplace/docs/teaching/eldercare_unit_1.pdf)

### Inferences
- In every country covered, publicly funded ESOL-for-work converges on the same sectors: **care and healthcare support, hospitality and catering, retail and customer service, and manufacturing or warehouse work**. These are high-volume entry-level jobs for migrants, mostly at Entry 3 to Level 1 (roughly A2–B1). A website targeting B1–C1 may need to bridge these up a level.
- Interview formats named in the curricula, beyond the face-to-face interview: **telephone interviews, tiered or panel interviews, mock interviews with feedback, and talking about references**. Video interviews appear through NHS recruiters and ESL Brains' online-interview lesson.

### Gaps
- No AMEP national curriculum document naming specific job sectors was found. PEP sector choices vary by provider.
- No single UK national ESOL employability standard was found. Content varies by provider.

## 5. Search-interest signals

### Takeaway
Generic interview-question searches are large: roughly 63,000 per month in the UK in 2025, according to a Ciphr Google study. The top intent is "questions to ask at the end of an interview". No reliable public keyword data was found for ESL-specific role phrases such as "English for nurses interview" or "hospitality interview English".

### Cited Findings
- Ciphr's Google study found about 63,000 UK searches per month related to "interview questions", or over 756,500 in the 12 months to December 2025. The top query was about questions to ask during or at the end of an interview (about 17,520 per month). Next came general advice on common questions and how to answer them (about 14,410 combined), then strengths and weaknesses (about 5,480). Smaller queries covered career goals (810) and salary (440). — [Ciphr press release](https://www.ciphr.com/press-releases/new-google-study-reveals-the-most-searched-for-interview-questions); [Ciphr infographic](https://www.ciphr.com/infographics/common-interview-questions-answered); [HR Grapevine, 2026-03-16](https://www.hrgrapevine.com/content/article/2026-03-16-what-756000-google-searches-reveal-about-candidate-anxiety); [FE News](https://www.fenews.co.uk/work-leadership/study-reveals-the-uks-most-googled-interview-questions)
- InterviewPal analysed 20,918 user-reported 2025 interview questions across tech, finance, healthcare, retail, education and early-career roles. It ranked "Tell me about yourself" first, then "Why are you interested in this role?" and "Walk me through your resume". AI and future-of-work questions increased sharply in 2025. The data is self-reported by the platform's users. — [InterviewPal](https://www.interviewpal.com/blog/the-top-100-most-asked-interview-questions-of-2025-ranked-by-frequency); [Methodology](https://blog.interviewpal.com/methodology-for-the-top-100-most-asked-interview-questions-of-2025)
- Small third-party keyword estimates for the US: "interview questions for technical jobs" about 140 per month, "technical interview questions with answers" about 90, and "questions for software engineer interview" about 30. These are low-confidence estimates from an SEO tool. — [seodata.dev technical interview questions](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/technical-interview-questions); [software engineer](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/questions-software-engineer-interview)

### Inferences
- An ESL interview site could rank topics by *question type* using the Ciphr data, in this order: questions to ask the interviewer, common questions with how to answer them, strengths and weaknesses, career goals, then salary. That ordering is evidence-backed.
- Role-specific ESL phrases are probably long-tail and low-volume, but likely to convert well. Their volumes were not verified.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends or keyword volumes were found for "English for nurses interview", "care worker interview questions", "hospitality interview English" or similar ESL phrases. A keyword tool (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush) would be needed.
- No breakdown of search volume by job role was found in the Ciphr data.

## 6. Gaps in free conversation-based (dialogue) materials at B1–C1

### Takeaway
Free material is plentiful for generic interview language and thin for **role-specific, dialogue-based interview lessons**. The clearest underserved combinations are care worker and healthcare assistant, nurse (competency-based NHS-style interviews), hospitality (waiter, barista, hotel front desk), retail and customer service, warehouse and logistics, and IT or software (behavioural plus technical). Underserved interview formats include phone screening, video or one-way video interviews, panel interviews, competency or STAR interviews, and salary negotiation.

### Cited Findings
- The search for hospitality or waiter interview ESL dialogues returned nothing specific to that role. The only hotel interview material found was the NorQuest CLB 3 listening task. — [NorQuest "Interview at a Hotel"](https://openeducationalberta.ca/norquest-lincworks-interviews-clb3/?p=64)
- No complete B2 software-developer interview ESL lesson was found. The material exists only as pieces: a Twee listening activity and EnglishPost sample answers. — [Twee](https://twee.com/esl-lesson-plans/job?page=2); [EnglishPost](https://englishpost.org/lesson-plans/lesson-tips-for-a-job-interview)
- Care-worker interview material is mostly native-speaker employer content (Randstad, Home Instead, High Speed Training). The ESL-specific caregiver interview guide found is a niche app blog, SpeakShark. — [Randstad](https://www.randstad.co.uk/career-advice/career-guidance/top-care-worker-interview-questions-answers-how-to-prepare-for-a-job/); [SpeakShark](https://speakshark.com/blog/caregiver-job-interview-english-practice-uk)
- Nursing interview guidance comes from OET and recruiters, in native-level English, focused on STAR and NHS values. It does not offer graded ESL dialogues. — [OET](https://oet.com/post/oet-career-interview-preparation); [ID Medical](https://www.id-medical.com/international-nurses-guide-to-preparing-for-your-nhs-nursing-interview-5-questions-with-example-answers/)
- Generic interview role-play frameworks exist, for example the Council of Europe scenario in which the teacher plays the interviewer first and learners then rotate roles. — [Council of Europe Tool 75](https://rm.coe.int/tool-75-scenario-preparing-for-a-job-interview/1680af055d)
- Telephone and tiered interviews appear in LINC curricula, and online interviews are covered by ESL Brains. Free dialogue material for phone screenings, panels and salary talk was not found in the material-site results. — [LINC 4](https://settlement.org/downloads/linc/LCG1to5/Tm9LINC4.pdf); [ESL Brains](https://eslbrains.com/?p=94360)

### Inferences
Below is a suggested evidence-weighted ranking for prioritising lesson ideas. It is the author's synthesis, not a measured ranking.

- **By role or sector:**
  1. Care worker and healthcare assistant: dominant in ESOL and IET, with little free ESL dialogue material.
  2. Nurse, internationally educated, in NHS-style competency and values interviews: high stakes, with documented anxiety and OET demand.
  3. Hospitality (waiter, hotel reception, kitchen): present in all four countries' ESOL provision, with almost no free dialogues.
  4. Retail and customer service.
  5. IT and software, behavioural plus explaining technical work: tutor and B2–C1 learner demand, with only fragmented materials.
  6. Manufacturing, warehouse and trades: IET demand, mostly at lower CEFR levels.
  7. Finance and business or office roles: already well served by business-English generic lessons and tutors.
- **By interview type:**
  1. Competency and behavioural (STAR).
  2. "Questions to ask the interviewer", the top search intent.
  3. Strengths and weaknesses, and tell me about yourself.
  4. Phone screening.
  5. Video and one-way recorded interviews.
  6. Panel or tiered interviews.
